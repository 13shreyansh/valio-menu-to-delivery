Your kitchen plans. Valio prepares the order.

For hotel and chain kitchens that buy from Valio Aimo. Say the dishes and guests, or a product and an amount. Valio does the recipe maths, checks live price and stock, and prepares the order. You approve only what your own rules send to you.

How it works

Opens Hotel Aurora, a sample kitchen. No password. Prices, stock and order-by times are live from Valio Aimo's Junction 2026 test system; orders are recorded on the account and nothing ships. Valio customers sign in with the company account set up by their Valio contact.

The order is the easy part. Getting to it is the busy work.

A head chef turns next week's menu into an order by hand: recipes times guests, minus what is on the shelf, rounded up to pack sizes, checked against stock and the budget. Then a wedding lands on Saturday and it all starts again.

  • Menu and bookings
  • Recipes × guests

  • Products and packs
  • Price, stock, order-by time

Valio already holds the catalogue, the prices and the stock. So Valio does these steps, and shows the maths behind every line.

The buyer sets three controls. They hold in every channel.

Spending limits

A weekly and a monthly limit, counted across every placed order: portal, Ask Valio and connected agents together. Over the limit, the order waits for a person or is refused, as the buyer chose.

Allergens and swaps

Products that contain or may contain a banned allergen are never ordered. A swap for a late product must keep the recipe working, add no allergen, and be approved first.

Approvals and the log

Changing a quantity after an OK reopens that OK. Every approval, rule change, refusal and order is written to the account log with who and when.

Open to the buyer's own purchasing agent

A hotel chain's agent connects to Valio over the Model Context Protocol with a per-account token. One call finds products, compares offers and places the order within its authority, or hands it to a person with a review link. It can never approve for them.
